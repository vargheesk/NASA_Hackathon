# Gravity Mind Game 🚀

![NASA](https://img.shields.io/badge/NASA-Spaces%20App%20Challenge-blue)

Welcome to **Gravity Mind Game**, a web-based game developed for the NASA Spaces App Challenge. Test your mental agility and quick-thinking skills by solving arithmetic expressions to survive in space!

## 🎮 How to Play

The rules of the game are simple:

1.  **Select the expression with the greater value.** You will be presented with two arithmetic expressions, and you have to choose the one that results in a higher number.
2.  **Use the up and down arrow keys or click on the expression** to make your selection.
3.  **Be quick!** You only have a short amount of time to answer each question.

## ✨ Features

* **Fast-Paced Gameplay:** A time limit of **1.5 seconds per question** keeps you on your toes.
* **Dynamic Expressions:** The game generates **random arithmetic expressions** using addition, subtraction, and multiplication, so every round is a new challenge.
* **Score Tracking:** Your score increases with every correct answer. See how high you can get!
* **Performance Metrics:** At the end of the game, you can see your **response times** for each question and your **average response time**.
* **Responsive Controls:** Play using either your **keyboard (up and down arrow keys) or your mouse**.
